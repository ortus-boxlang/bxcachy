# Theme credits

The ten themes `catppuccin`, `catppuccin-latte`, `tokyo-night`, `gruvbox`, `nord`, `rose-pine`, `kanagawa`, `everforest`, `matte-black` and
`flexoki-light` are copied from Omarchy (https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy, `themes/`): their `colors.toml` palettes and `neovim.lua` plugin
specs. Omarchy is MIT licensed, Copyright (c) David Heinemeier Hansson. The colour schemes themselves belong to their original authors.

The five `bxcachy*` themes are BxCachy's own, in the same format (`colors.toml` plus `neovim.lua`), so themes move both ways between Omarchy and BxCachy.
